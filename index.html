03 / About

09 / How I Work

From business challenge to usable product.

I work across the product lifecycle, combining discovery, architecture, delivery and continuous improvement. The goal is not simply to launch a website or application, but to make it useful, maintainable and easy to improve.

01 / Discover

Understand the problem

Map user needs, business processes, constraints and success criteria before deciding on technology.

02 / Design

Shape the right solution

Define product scope, information architecture, workflows and responsive interfaces.

03 / Build

Develop and iterate

Implement practical web solutions, prioritize maintainability and validate real user journeys.

04 / Improve

Measure what matters

Review usability, search discoverability, technical quality and operational feedback to guide the next iteration.
